About KRUX STUDY

KRUX STUDY brings ten essential everyday academic utilities into one calm, high-contrast workspace designed for students across high school and university.

Instead of juggling bloated apps, login walls, and slow dashboards, every tool in KRUX STUDY runs locally in your browser so you can plan sessions, calculate grades, track attendance, and organize notes immediately.

Privacy Policy

KRUX STUDY does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. There are no accounts, no analytics trackers, and no third-party cookies.

All tasks, exams, GPA entries, assignments, and notes are stored strictly on your device using browser LocalStorage. You remain in complete control and can erase your saved data at any time.
